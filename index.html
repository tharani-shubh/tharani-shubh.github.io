Fusball Privacy Policy

Prepared 10 November 2026. Publisher: KashIn Games. Contact: kashingames28@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

Fusball stores your game settings, tutorial completion, level, currency balances, unlocked items and purchase-processing history on your device. The game does not currently require an account or provide an online multiplayer service. Local multiplayer uses one device.

Ads and privacy choices

Fusball integrates Google Mobile Ads for rewarded ads and other ad placements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain their services. Google’s User Messaging Platform presents consent choices where required. Where available, use “Ad privacy” in the currency shop to revisit advertising privacy choices.

Learn more about Google advertising data use and Google’s Privacy Policy. Ad availability and privacy controls depend on your region and the configured services.

Purchases

Optional purchases use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information under its own terms. Fusball receives purchase details, including product identifiers, purchase tokens and signed purchase records, to verify purchases, grant items and avoid duplicate grants. The game does not receive your complete payment-card details.

Diagnostics

The game includes optional Sentry error reporting. When enabled in the distributed release, error reports can include exception details, stack traces, app version and technical device or runtime information. The integration disables default personal information and local-variable capture. Sentry’s Privacy Policy describes its service practices. The publisher must confirm whether reporting is enabled and document applicable retention before publication.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local progress and purchase-processing history remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Clearing local data removes saved progress and may affect locally recorded purchased currency and items. Google and other service providers retain their own records according to their policies and applicable obligations; uninstalling the game does not delete those records.

Contact kashingames28@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests involving information held by KashIn Games. Information controlled by Google or other service providers may require a request to that provider.

Audience and policy changes

The publisher must confirm the intended age audience and any additional child-directed requirements before release. If game features or data practices change, this policy will be updated with the relevant effective date.
